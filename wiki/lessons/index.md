# Lessons

What went wrong or right, and why — the mistakes worth not repeating and the moves worth repeating. Lessons record the cause, not just the outcome.

- [A Passing Suite Can Test the Wrong Thing](/wiki/lessons/a-passing-suite-can-test-the-wrong-thing.md) — Tests that define their own fixtures and constants can pass while the real configuration is wrong; verify at least once through the real path.
